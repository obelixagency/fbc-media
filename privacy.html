Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

"Family Brain Clash Automation" (the "App") is an internal publishing tool operated by OBELIX Agency for the Family Brain Clash channels. It is used only by the channel owner to upload and manage the channel's own videos.

What data the App accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): uploading videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, posting the channel's own comment on its own videos, and reading the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, average view percentage, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Facebook and Instagram (via the Meta Graph API): publishing the channel's own Reels and videos and reading their insights.

The App does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or any other users. It does not sell or share data with third parties. Aggregated performance numbers of our own videos are stored privately to decide which quiz formats to make next.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted as repository secrets and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Analytics numbers are kept only as long as needed to plan content.
